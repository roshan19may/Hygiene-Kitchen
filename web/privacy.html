Privacy Policy

KitchenLens — Food Safety & Hygiene Verification · Last updated

KitchenLens is operated by , ("we"). KitchenLens helps cloud kitchens and restaurants record their hygiene process and share it with customers through a QR seal on each delivery bag. This policy explains what data the KitchenLens apps (Android and iPhone) and the QR report page handle, why, and how you can delete it.

Who uses KitchenLens

The apps are for kitchen businesses and their staff (aged 18 or over). Customers who scan a QR seal do not need an account and do not give us any personal information.

The apps use the phone camera only when staff tap a record button. Clips are recorded without audio. We do not use advertising, advertising IDs, contact lists or location tracking, and we do not sell data.

Staff in photos and clips

Kitchen staff may appear in hygiene clips. The kitchen business is responsible for informing its staff and obtaining their consent before recording, as required by India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Where data is stored

  • Google Firebase (Google Cloud, Mumbai region): accounts, records, photos and clips.
  • Cloudinary: only when a kitchen connects its own Cloudinary account in the app, photos and clips are stored in that account.
  • Phone storage: drafts and, in demo mode, all records stay on the phone.
  • The QR report page is hosted on GitHub Pages or Firebase Hosting, which keep standard access logs.

How long we keep data

Data is kept while the kitchen account is active. A kitchen can delete its account at any time, which deletes its profile, records, photos, clips, orders and seals within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Kitchen details (shop icon) → Delete my account and data. Without the app, use the account deletion request page.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by writing to us. We reply within 30 days.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Access rules allow each kitchen to change only its own records, and hygiene records cannot be edited after saving.
