Delete your KitchenLens account

KitchenLens — Food Safety & Hygiene Verification, by

Fastest: in the app

  1. Open KitchenLens and sign in.
  2. Tap the shop icon at the top of the Home screen (Kitchen details).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my account and data.
  4. Enter your password, type DELETE and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Without the app

Email us from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my KitchenLens account":

We confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and kitchen profile (name, FSSAI and GST numbers).
  • All hygiene records, photos, video clips and supplier bill photos.
  • All orders and QR seals. Seals already given to customers stop working.

What may be kept

Only records the law requires us to keep, for the period it requires. If you connected your own Cloudinary account, delete those files in Cloudinary.
